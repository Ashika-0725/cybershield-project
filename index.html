Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Fake emails or messages that trick you into sharing passwords or clicking harmful links.

Malware

Harmful software such as viruses, spyware and ransomware that damages or locks your files.

Ransomware

Encrypts your data and demands payment. Regular offline backups are the best defence.

Social Engineering

Manipulating people, not machines, into giving away confidential information.

Weak Passwords

Short or reused passwords are easily guessed or cracked by automated tools.

Public Wi‑Fi Risks

Open networks can let others snoop on traffic. Avoid sensitive logins on them.

Password Strength Checker

Type a password to test it. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

Strength: –
  • At least 12 characters
  • A lowercase letter
  • An uppercase letter
  • A number
  • A symbol (e.g. ! @ # $)
  • Not a common password
